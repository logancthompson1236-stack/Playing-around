Controls

Left thumb anywhere on the left half: floating stick. Push to steer.

FIRE twin cannons (aim assist helps near targets). Slide your thumb between buttons without lifting it.

BOOST burns the yellow meter. BRAKE for tight turns.

Keep an enemy in the lock ring until it reads LOCK: your cannon bolts turn gold and curve toward it, and Seeker missiles home in on it.

EMP stuns nearby enemies for a few seconds and erases incoming fire. Stunned ships can't shoot and take extra damage.

Tap the radar to toggle the full sector map.

Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Space fire, Shift boost, L brake, E missile, Q EMP, P pause.

Objective

Clear 3 waves of hostiles, then fly through the warp gate. Pick an upgrade and push deeper. Every cleared wave drops supplies just ahead of you.

Shoot big asteroids to split them. Orange volatile rocks explode and hurt everything nearby. Cyan crystal rocks drop power-ups.
