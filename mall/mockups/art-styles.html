Mall Revival

Art style test: the same corner of the mall, drawn two ways.

Drag to turn the camera

2D drawn

Flat shapes with bold outlines, drawn in code and turned into sprites when the game loads. Sharp on any screen and a tiny download. If you hire an artist later, their sprites can replace any piece without changing the game.

3D low-poly

Simple 3D shapes with cartoon shading and real shadows. The lighting does a lot of the polish, and the camera can turn and zoom. It reads more like a toy diorama than a drawing.

In the scene: you at the Sweet Tooth register, a boarded-up unit, Pixel Palace Arcade, the fountain, a customer waiting at Pretzel Pals, another waiting at Dollar Dreams with nobody at the register, a shuttered unit and a closed wing. Gus is mopping out front.
