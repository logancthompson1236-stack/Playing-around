Mall Revival Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Mall Revival is a single-player game. This policy covers the Android app and the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. Mall Revival does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases, and it does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Your saved game

Your mall and your settings are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device. Android's own backup (run by Google, under your Google account settings) may include the save if you have device backup turned on. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes the save.

Internet use

The game plays fully offline and does not send anything over the internet.

Children

Mall Revival is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if the game adds online features), this page will be updated before the change ships, with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/logancthompson1236-stack/Playing-around/issues.
